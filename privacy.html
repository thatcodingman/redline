Docket / Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026. This page covers what Redline collects when you visit this site or use its forms, and why.

Analytics

Redline uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they read, and roughly where traffic comes from. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects standard technical data — your approximate location (from IP address), device and browser type, and which pages you visited. Redline does not use this data to identify you individually.

Forms

Redline uses Netlify Forms to handle the forms on this site. Whatever you submit is stored in Redline's Netlify account and is not sold or shared with third parties. Specifically:

  • Email signup — your email address is used only to send the twice-monthly docket email. To stop receiving it, use the contact form.
  • Submit a Filing — the company, link, date, and description you provide are used only to research and verify the potential filing. Your email, if you leave one, is used only to follow up on that tip.
  • Contact form — your name (if given), email, and message are used only to respond to you.

Cookies

This site currently sets cookies for analytics only, as described above. Redline does not currently display third-party advertising. If that changes, this policy will be updated to disclose the ad network(s) used, what additional cookies they set, and how to opt out of personalized ads.

Fonts

This site loads typefaces (Libre Baskerville, Courier Prime) from Google Fonts' servers, which may log standard request data such as your IP address, separately from Google Analytics.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Netlify, which processes standard web server logs (IP address, request time, pages requested) as part of serving the site.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and Redline does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Your rights

You can ask what data Redline has about you, ask for it to be deleted, or unsubscribe from the docket email at any time — use the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters — new third-party services, advertising, or data uses — this page will be updated and the date above will change.
