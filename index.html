REDLINE-2026-011 · Privacy Statement Amendment

PayPal — Privacy Statement

Scope: U.S. PayPal Privacy Statement (PayPal account and non-account users). Venmo is explicitly excluded — this is a PayPal-only change.
Document & Effective Date: Sept 28, 2026
Verified by Redline: Sept 30, 2026
Why this status: PayPal's own Policy Updates notice directly quotes the exact language describing what changed, and Redline independently retrieved the current live Privacy Statement to confirm the Sept 28, 2026 date. This resolves a candidate first flagged as held on Sept 19, 2026, when the document showed an unresolved Jul 6, 2026 revision date.
What Changed

PayPal's Sept 28, 2026 Privacy Statement amendment added clarifying language about how personal data is handled for PayPal World and third-party-wallet transactions, added language describing PayPal's obligations regarding disclosures to Authorities, and removed outdated affiliate-sharing disclosures from the California, North Dakota, and Vermont Supplemental Financial Privacy Notices. The current document also contains AI/Automated Decision Making language (e.g. for risk, fraud, and creditworthiness assessments), but Redline could not confirm whether that language was newly added in this revision or carried over unchanged — it is not attributed to this change.

Before — Pre-Sept 28, 2026

Per PayPal's own change notice: state supplemental financial privacy notices for California, North Dakota, and Vermont included affiliate-sharing disclosures later described as outdated. No clean archived copy of the exact prior Privacy Statement was located to confirm the full prior wording.

After — Effective Sept 28, 2026

Adds PayPal World and third-party-wallet data-handling language and Authorities-disclosure language; removes the now-outdated CA/ND/VT affiliate-sharing disclosures.

Why It Matters

This is a case where the held/hold discipline paid off: rather than guess at what the unresolved Jul 6, 2026 date meant, Redline waited for PayPal's own account of the change and confirmed it directly before publishing — avoiding a filing based on a stale or misread document date.

Who Is Affected

U.S. PayPal account and non-account personal-information holders covered by the U.S. Privacy Statement. Venmo is a separately governed, excluded service.

Notice Given
YES — CLEAR

Via PayPal's own Policy Updates notice, which directly describes the change.

Community report

No independent news coverage of this specific Sept 28 amendment was found at the time of writing — PayPal's own notice is the only account located.

REDLINE-2026-012 · Digital Agent Restrictions

Best Buy — Terms and Conditions

Scope: U.S. Best Buy Properties — BestBuy.com, mobile site, Android/iOS apps, and related purchase/financing/support/account services
Document Date: Sept 25, 2026 (no separate effective date stated)
Verified by Redline: Sept 30, 2026
Why this status: Redline retrieved the current live document directly and confirmed both the Sept 25, 2026 date and the exact Digital Agent section text. This is Partially Verified rather than Verified because Redline has not retrieved and diffed the immediately preceding version — an independent archival tracker (ConductAtlas) corroborates that a revision occurred around this date, but its sentence-count data doesn't establish which specific provisions were added versus carried over.
What Changed

Best Buy's Terms and Conditions, last updated Sept 25, 2026, now contain a "Digital Agents" section defining a Digital Agent as any AI-enabled technology able to take autonomous or semi-autonomous action on a person's behalf. The section requires Digital Agents to identify themselves as such in all requests, operate only through Best Buy's authorized methods, avoid mimicking human behavior or circumventing security measures like CAPTCHAs, and be used only by authenticated, logged-in customers. Best Buy reserves the right to limit, restrict, suspend, block, or ban any Digital Agent at its discretion. An independent archival tracker recorded 19 sentences added, 2 removed, and 38 modified around this date, but Redline could not confirm whether the Digital Agent section specifically was introduced in this revision or existed earlier — it is described here as current content, not attributed to this specific change.

Before — Prior version

Not yet independently retrieved by Redline. An archival tracker records a revision around Sept 25–26, 2026, but the exact prior wording is unconfirmed.

After — Current, dated Sept 25, 2026

Defines "Digital Agent," requires self-identification and authorized-method-only interaction, bars mimicking human behavior or bypassing security checks, restricts use to authenticated customers, and reserves Best Buy's right to block any Digital Agent at will.

Why It Matters

As AI shopping agents and browser assistants become more common, this is one of the more detailed Digital Agent clauses Redline has seen — it's worth watching whether other retailers adopt similar identify-yourself-and-behave-like-a-bot language, and whether "Best Buy's authorized methods" ends up meaning an official API versus a blanket ban on unsanctioned agents.

Who Is Affected

All Best Buy Properties users — purchases, financing, support, accounts, and guest use — and any AI agent or automated tool acting on a customer's behalf.

Notice Given
NO CLEAR NOTICE LOCATED

The current Terms state Best Buy may change them at any time without notice; no separate announcement was located.

Community report

This candidate was originally flagged by a routine monthly monitoring pass via an independent archival policy-tracking service (ConductAtlas), which recorded the sentence-level change counts. Redline did not find dedicated community or press discussion of this specific revision at the time of writing.
