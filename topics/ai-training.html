Docket / Topics / AI Training & Data Use Changes

Every Redline filing where a company's Terms of Service or Privacy Policy changed how customer or user data may be used to train AI models, or added new AI-related restrictions. Listed in order filed, most recent first.

These changes decide whether your content, prompts, or account data can be used to train a company's own AI models or a third party's, and whether that's opt-in, opt-out, or not disclosed at all.

  1. Best Buy · REDLINE-2026-012 · Sept 25, 2026 · Partially Verified
    Best Buy Terms and Conditions: New Rules for AI "Digital Agents"

    Best Buy's Terms require AI agents to self-identify, use authorized methods only, and let Best Buy block them at will.

  2. Adobe · REDLINE-2026-010 · Oct 3, 2025 · Partially Verified
    Adobe General Terms of Use: Full Replacement on Oct 3, 2025

    Adobe replaced its entire General Terms of Use, adding a ban on training competing AI with Adobe outputs and broad service-change limits.

  3. LinkedIn · REDLINE-2026-008 · Nov 3, 2025 · Partially Verified
    LinkedIn User Agreement & Privacy Policy: AI Training Expanded to More Regions

    LinkedIn uses member data in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada and Hong Kong to train generative AI, opt-out by default.

  4. GitHub Copilot · REDLINE-2026-006 · Apr 24, 2026 · Verified
    GitHub Copilot Privacy Statement: AI Training Flipped to Opt-Out

    GitHub trains AI on interaction data from individual Copilot plans unless users opt out. Business and Enterprise plans excluded.

  5. Starlink · REDLINE-2026-005 · Jan 15, 2026 · Partially Verified
    Starlink Privacy Policy: AI Training Added, Opt-Out by Default

    Starlink's Global Privacy Policy lets it train AI models on customer data unless users opt out.

  6. Reddit · REDLINE-2026-004 · Jul 1, 2026 · Partially Verified
    Reddit Privacy Policy Now Names LLM Providers as Data Recipients

    Reddit's May 26, 2026 Privacy Policy revision names LLM providers as recipients of public content, with no notice found.

  7. Adobe · REDLINE-2026-002 · Jun 18, 2024 · Verified
    Adobe Terms of Use: AI Training Backlash and the June 2024 Revision

    Adobe's June 2024 Terms of Use update sparked backlash over AI training; a Jun 18 revision added an explicit no-generative-AI-training statement.

  8. Google Ads · REDLINE-2026-001 · Jul 1, 2026 · Verified
    Google Ads Terms of Service: Arbitration, AI Inputs & Fees Added

    Google Ads' first Terms of Service rewrite in eight years adds binding arbitration, AI use of advertiser inputs and regulatory fees.
