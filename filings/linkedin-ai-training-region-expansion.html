REDLINE-2026-008 · AI Training Region Expansion

LinkedIn — User Agreement & Privacy Policy

Scope: LinkedIn members in EEA, Switzerland, UK, Canada, and Hong Kong (GenAI training); Microsoft affiliate ad-data sharing expansion is separately region-specific. No new AI-training change stated for U.S. members.
Document & Effective Date: Nov 3, 2025
Verified by Redline: Sept 1, 2026
Why this status: LinkedIn's own Help Center page directly confirms the change, corroborated by seven independent outlets. Redline has not yet independently retrieved LinkedIn's September 2024 prior-version documents to diff the exact regional and affiliate-sharing language line by line.
What Changed

Effective November 3, 2025, LinkedIn expanded the regions where member profile data and public content can be used to train generative AI models to include the EEA, Switzerland, UK, Canada, and Hong Kong, and separately expanded the scope of member data shared with Microsoft for ad personalization in specified regions. Both changes are opt-out — enabled by default under "legitimate interest," with a toggle in Settings > Data Privacy to disable. LinkedIn states opting out does not undo training that already occurred. LinkedIn's own U.S.-specific notice states there is no new AI-training change for U.S. members from this update.

Before — Sept 2024 scope

GenAI training language and an opt-out applied to a narrower set of regions, per the September 2024 update.

After — Effective Nov 3, 2025

Expanded to use profile details and public content from EEA, Switzerland, UK, Canada, and Hong Kong members to train AI models, and to share more member data with Microsoft for advertising. Private messages remain excluded from both.

Why It Matters

This is a genuine expansion of AI-training scope for millions of additional members, but it was clearly announced by LinkedIn's own account — the more notable detail is that opting out doesn't retroactively remove data already used for training, meaning the practical window to opt out is before, not after, the effective date.

Who Is Affected

LinkedIn members in EEA, Switzerland, UK, Canada, and Hong Kong for AI training; a broader regional scope applies for ad-data sharing with Microsoft.

Notice Given
YES — CLEAR

Coverage was widespread and consistent across privacy-focused outlets (Help Net Security, MediaNama) and general tech press, all converging on the same effective date, regional scope, and opt-out mechanism — an unusually high degree of independent corroboration for a policy change.
