REDLINE-2026-006 · AI Training Default Flip

GitHub Copilot — Privacy Statement

Scope: Copilot Free, Pro, Pro+, and Max individual plans only — Business and Enterprise excluded
Announced: Mar 25, 2026
Effective: Apr 24, 2026
Verified by Redline: Sept 1, 2026
Why this status: GitHub's own blog post and documentation confirm the policy, its scope, and its effective date directly.
What Changed

GitHub flipped individual-plan Copilot data training from opt-in to opt-out by default: inputs, outputs, code snippets, and session context from Free, Pro, and Pro+ accounts now feed AI model training automatically unless the user disables it in Copilot settings. Business and Enterprise plans are explicitly excluded.

Before — Pre-Apr 24, 2026

Interaction data from individual Copilot plans previously required opt-in consent to train AI models.

After — Effective Apr 24, 2026

Interaction data will be used to train and improve AI models unless the user opts out. Copilot Business and Enterprise plans are unaffected.

Why It Matters

Flipping a default from opt-in to opt-out means inactive users are automatically enrolled. Multiple outlets reported GitHub's notification email didn't link directly to the opt-out toggle, adding friction to actually exercising the choice — this one was announced, but the mechanics of opting out weren't made easy.

Who Is Affected

Individual Copilot Free / Pro / Pro+ / Max subscribers. Business and Enterprise plans excluded.

Notice Given
YES — CLEAR

GitHub Blog post and Docs update, ~30 days ahead of the effective date.

Community report

GitHub's own community forum saw active discussion, including one thread with 146+ participants, with a recurring complaint that the notification email didn't link directly to the opt-out setting. Competitor GitLab published a blog post framing the shift as a governance concern for regulated industries evaluating AI coding tools.

— GitHub Community forum, GitLab blog; not an official GitHub statement
