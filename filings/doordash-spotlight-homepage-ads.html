DoorDash — Consumer App Homepage

Scope: DoorDash consumer marketplace homepage (not the Dasher driver app)
Policy Type: App Update / Ad Placement (not a Terms/Privacy filing)
Announced: Jun 8, 2026
Effective: Jun 8, 2026 (separate rollout schedule not stated)
Why this status: DoorDash's own advertising and corporate pages directly confirm the new ad format and its stated purpose of replacing homepage banners.
Evidence level: Vendor announcement Archived comparison — not yet retrieved
Before/After comparison Pending — no pre-Jun-8, 2026 homepage build or screenshot has been retrieved yet. DoorDash's own advertising pages (linked below) show real creative examples of Spotlight in place — readers can view those directly, though Redline has not independently retrieved a pre-launch homepage screenshot to compare against.
What we know

DoorDash announced Spotlight on Jun 8, 2026, stated it replaces homepage banners, and reported (in its own investor press release) 2x higher click-through rates than banner ads in early testing.

What we don't know

The exact visual size difference versus the prior banner format, whether the April 2026 Reddit report of increased pre-listing ads is related, and how "early testing" for the 2x CTR figure was measured or over what period.

DoorDash Ads announcement — Ad surface change
DoorDash's homepage used standard banner adsnow features "Spotlight," a large-format homepage ad unit using static or video creative, explicitly replacing the prior homepage banners.
What changed

DoorDash introduced "Spotlight" on June 8, 2026 — a large homepage advertising placement that explicitly replaces the previous homepage banner ad format, per DoorDash's own advertising and corporate announcement pages. DoorDash's investor relations press release states Spotlight delivered 2x higher click-through rates than banners in early testing.

What did NOT change (as far as Redline has confirmed)

This report does not establish any change to DoorDash's pricing, delivery fees, or Terms of Service. A separate, unconfirmed community report about increased ads above restaurant listings is held below rather than merged into this record.

Community report

Separately, a Reddit user reported in April 2026 that more ads appeared above restaurant listings — but that report predates Spotlight's June 8 announcement and hasn't been officially confirmed as connected to it. Kept as its own held lead below rather than merged into this report. Independent trade press (Marketing Dive) corroborated the Spotlight launch and its stated performance claims without adding new figures of its own.

— Reddit user report; Marketing Dive; not an official DoorDash statement except where cited
Why it matters

A named, larger ad format replacing a smaller one — one DoorDash itself says outperforms the old format 2x on click-through — represents a deliberate, measured increase in advertising surface on the homepage, not an incidental one. That's a concrete, permanent record worth keeping even though DoorDash announced it clearly.
