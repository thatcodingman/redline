REDLINE-2026-009 · Cross-Platform Contact Discovery

PayPal / Venmo — Privacy Statement

Scope: U.S. Venmo personal accounts and PayPal personal accounts; Teen Accounts are handled under separate provisions
Document Date: announced ahead of launch, Sept 30, 2025
Effective Date: Nov 17, 2025 (Venmo Privacy Statement)
Verified by Redline: Sept 1, 2026
Why this status: PayPal's own "Policy Updates" archive page directly quotes the exact language describing the change and its default-on nature, and Redline retrieved Venmo's current, dated Privacy Statement directly to confirm the effective date.
What Changed

Starting November 2025, Venmo and PayPal users can find and pay each other using contact information, primarily phone number at launch. PayPal's own pre-launch notice states plainly that being discoverable this way requires no action — it's the default — and that users who don't want to be found this way must change their settings.

Before — Pre-Nov 2025

Venmo and PayPal accounts could not be found or paid across platforms using contact information.

After — Effective Nov 17, 2025

Accounts can be found and paid by users on the other platform via phone number (and later email), with matched accounts able to see name, username, profile photo, and country by default, unless the user changes their Privacy settings.

Why It Matters

Default-on discoverability across a combined 2-billion-user network is a meaningful privacy exposure increase, particularly because it was rolled out as a convenience feature rather than framed as a privacy change — users have to already know to look for the "Find Me" setting to opt out before their number becomes a cross-platform lookup key.

Who Is Affected

U.S. Venmo and PayPal personal-account holders. Teen Accounts are handled under separate provisions.

Notice Given
YES — CLEAR

Via PayPal's own policy-update archive and in-app/email notices.

Community report

Tech press (TechCrunch, CNET, 9to5Mac) covered the feature primarily as a convenience update, but multiple outlets specifically flagged the default-searchable setting as a scam/spam risk worth disabling before the feature went live, since a phone number is enough to locate an account across platforms.

— TechCrunch, CNET, 9to5Mac; not official PayPal or Venmo statements
