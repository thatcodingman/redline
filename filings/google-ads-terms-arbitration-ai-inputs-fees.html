Google Ads — Terms of Service

Scope: Google Ads advertiser accounts (all regions); does not cover other Google product terms
Published by Google: Jun 1, 2026
Effective: Jul 1, 2026
Verified by Redline: Sept 1, 2026
Why this status: Primary Google support documentation confirms the policy and effective date directly, corroborated by multiple independent trade-press reports.
What Changed

Google's first Google Ads Terms of Service rewrite in eight years pulls advertiser inputs (e.g. text typed into Ads Advisor) into its systems for improving ad performance, replaces the prior dispute process with arbitration, adds a clause permitting jurisdiction-specific regulatory fees as separate invoice line items, and narrows the liability cap to a per-account basis.

Before — Pre-Jul 2026 Terms

Advertiser inputs into conversational features were not addressed. Disputes followed standard venue-based resolution. The liability cap applied on a total-account-spend basis.

After — Effective Jul 1, 2026

Advertiser inputs may now be used across Google Ads features to improve campaign performance. Disputes move to binding arbitration under AAA rules with a 30-day opt-out. The liability cap shifts to a narrower per-account basis.

Why It Matters

A per-account liability cap limits recovery if a platform-wide error affects many advertisers at once — a narrower ceiling than the total-spend basis it replaces.

Who Is Affected

All Google Ads advertisers, globally. Does not cover other Google product terms.

Notice Given
YES — CLEAR

Email sent Jun 1, 2026, ahead of the Jul 1 effective date. Arbitration opt-out available within a limited post-effective window.

Community report

Ad-ops and marketing outlets tie the arbitration overhaul to a wave of mass-arbitration claims filed against Google Ads in May 2026, and advise advertisers to watch upcoming invoices closely for new regulatory-fee line items.

— Webtopia, ALM Corp coverage; not an official Google statement
