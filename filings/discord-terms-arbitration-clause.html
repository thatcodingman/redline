What Changed

A third-party document tracker records a change to Discord's Terms of Service dated May 12, 2026. Redline has confirmed the current terms directly but has not yet located and diffed the specific pre-change version — this filing is published as Partially Verified rather than Verified for that reason.

Before — Pre-May 12, 2026

Not yet independently retrieved by Redline. A third-party tracker flags this date as when a revision occurred, but the exact prior wording is unconfirmed.

After — Current Terms

Mandatory arbitration for U.S. residents with a 30-day opt-out by email, venue for non-arbitrable disputes in New Castle County, Delaware, and a mediation step once 10% of a mass-filing batch resolves.

Why It Matters

Discord's own arbitration clause states that a material change restarts a 30-day opt-out window. If this May 2026 update was material, users who opted out under a prior version may need to opt out again to stay out of arbitration.

Who Is Affected

U.S.-based Discord users. EU/UK users are carved out of arbitration under separate terms.

Notice Given
UNKNOWN

Not independently confirmed by Redline for this specific update.

Community report

A third-party document tracker (ConductAtlas) flagged the May 12, 2026 revision date. Redline did not find dedicated community discussion threads specific to this particular update at the time of writing — a contrast to Discord's 2018 and 2024 arbitration changes, both of which drew substantial user pushback and press coverage.
