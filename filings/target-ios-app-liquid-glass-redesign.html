What we know

Target announced the Liquid Glass redesign on Aug 6, 2026, directly in its own App Store release notes. Separately, Apple requires all iOS apps to fully support Liquid Glass by September 2026.

What we don't know

The exact visual differences from the prior build, whether Android received the same redesign at the same time, and whether Target's update was a discretionary redesign choice or primarily compliance with Apple's platform-wide deadline.

App Store release notes
Target's iOS app previous interfacereceived a refreshed look and feel built on Apple's "Liquid Glass" design language, per the company's own release notes.
What changed

Target's App Store release notes for the August 6, 2026 update describe a visual redesign adopting Apple's Liquid Glass design system. This is a UI/UX change, not a change to Target's Terms of Service or Privacy Policy.

What did NOT change (as far as Redline has confirmed)

This report does not establish any change to ads, permissions, pricing, or cancellation flows. Only the visual interface is confirmed as changed — a separate, still-held lead below covers Target's sponsored placements.

Community report

No dedicated community discussion thread specifically about Target's redesign was located. Separately, Apple requires all iOS apps to fully support Liquid Glass by September 2026, and industry coverage of Apple's own "Liquid Glass Design Gallery" — which showcases roughly a dozen third-party apps that redesigned around the new system — does not include Target among the featured apps.

— 9to5Mac, Gadget Hacks, SpotMe developer documentation; not an official Target statement
Why it matters

Because Apple mandates Liquid Glass support platform-wide by September 2026, Target's August 6 update likely reflects OS-level compliance rather than a discretionary Target redesign choice — which is a meaningfully different story than "Target chose to redesign." Redline is downgrading how much editorial weight this report carries for that reason; it remains useful mainly as a dated record of when the change reached Target's app.
