Redline has filed two changes to PayPal's privacy documents, oldest first. The first covers cross-platform contact discovery by phone number across PayPal and Venmo. The second is PayPal's Sept 28, 2026 U.S. Privacy Statement amendment, which is PayPal-only. Each links to the full filing with before-and-after language, sources and a confidence rating.

  1. REDLINE-2026-009 · Nov 17, 2025 · Verified
    PayPal & Venmo Privacy Statement: Cross-Platform Contact Discovery On by Default

    PayPal and Venmo users can be found and paid across platforms by phone number, visible by default unless Privacy settings change.

  2. REDLINE-2026-011 · Sept 28, 2026 · Verified
    PayPal Privacy Statement Amendment, Sept 28, 2026

    PayPal's Sept 28, 2026 Privacy Statement amendment adds PayPal World, third-party wallet and Authorities-disclosure language.
